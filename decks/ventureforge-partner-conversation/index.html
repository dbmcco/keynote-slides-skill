Situation and complication

A client can feel the operational pain before they can justify a project.

That gap stalls good conversations: the pain is real, but the case is not ready for a scope, budget, or board-level decision.

Data ask

Ask for what they already have.

The client does not need a perfect package. They need enough signal for Braydon to confirm the analysis can start.

What do you personally want from this?
What does the organization keep struggling with?
The source says to be specific and not wait for the client to figure out what to send. The two questions matter because they separate the stakeholder win from the organizational win.
Operating model

The partner owns the relationship. LFW owns the analysis.

This is the trust move: the partner stays the lead, while LFW supplies the artifact the partner can walk through.

Make the boundary explicit. This protects the partner relationship while making LFW's role concrete. Braydon can join the client walkthrough when helpful, but the partner remains the relationship lead.
Client artifact

The report answers four questions.

The format can evolve. The client-facing job is stable: show what the data says, what it is worth, and what should happen next.

VentureForge report
Problems identified
Ranked by likely impact.
Opportunities quantified
What the gaps cost and what solving them is worth.
ROE frame
Before/after case with baselines where possible.
Honest recommendation
What is worth solving, and what is not a fit.
This is the core value artifact. The report should help the client decide whether there is a compelling case for software. Do not overspecify the report format; the source says the format is evolving.
Trust mechanics

Free works because the report earns the next step.

The client signs an NDA, sends usable context, and gets an independent read. If the case is not compelling, the client still learned something useful.

The catch?
There is no purchase obligation. The NDA protects the data before any forward motion.
Who sees the data?
Only the LFW analysis team, under the NDA and for the analysis purpose.
How long?
One week from LFW confirming there is enough to work with.
This slide should reduce skepticism. The source explicitly frames the analysis as free and independent. The safest phrasing is confidence, not pressure: LFW is confident the report will reveal whether there is a real case.
After the report

The report is not the end state. It is the bridge from fuzzy opportunity to a software delivery path.
