# Slides

## 1. VentureForge

VentureForge is the partner-friendly front door into LightForge Works: client
data and context become a one-week problem/opportunity report.

## 2. The 30-Second Explanation

Keep the talk track simple. LFW analyzes what the client already has and returns
a report that shows what is worth solving.

## 3. Why Partners Need It

Use VentureForge when the client feels the pain but cannot quantify it, needs
CFO/VP-level justification, or has a stalled deal because the case is not strong
enough.

## 4. What To Ask For

Ask for data extracts and problem context. The client does not need a perfect
package; Braydon confirms when there is enough.

## 5. The Two Wins

Capture the stakeholder win and the organizational win before analysis starts.

## 6. Handoff Model

The partner owns the relationship. Braydon runs the analysis and produces the
report. The partner leads the follow-up conversation.

## 7. What The Report Contains

Problems identified, opportunities quantified, ROE framing, and an honest
recommendation.

## 8. Why It Is Free

The analysis is free because LFW is confident the work will reveal whether
there is a real case. If not, the honest answer is still useful.

## 9. After The Report

When the case is compelling, move into the existing ForgeWorks process:
discovery, mockups, working software, fixed scope and price.

## 10. The Next Decision

Use the next partner meeting to pick one client, make the data ask, and run the
first VentureForge report.

## 11. Source Discipline

Separate what is known from source material, what the deck infers as
positioning, and what still needs validation.

## 12. Close

VentureForge adds a front door: one client, one data ask, one decision-ready
report.
