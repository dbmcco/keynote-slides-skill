# Brief

## Goal

Create a partner-facing alignment deck that makes VentureForge easy for Justin,
Brendan, and consulting partners to explain in a client conversation.

## Audience

Partners who own client trust and need a simple way to introduce LightForge
Works as the analysis and delivery layer behind an operational software
opportunity.

## Narrative

1. Clients often know something is broken before they can quantify it.
2. VentureForge gives partners a low-friction front door: data and context in,
   a one-week report out.
3. The report identifies problems, quantifies opportunities, frames return on
   effort, and recommends what is or is not worth solving.
4. If the report shows a case, the client moves into the existing ForgeWorks
   path: discovery, mockups, working software, fixed monthly scope.
5. The partner keeps the relationship; Braydon and LFW run the analysis and
   produce the report.

## Key Proof Points

- Primary source says the analysis is free, NDA-backed, and independent of any
  purchase decision.
- The handoff starts only when Braydon confirms there is enough data/context for
  the one-week clock.
- VentureForge is positioned as a front door to ForgeWorks, not as a generic AI
  platform pitch.
- Folio North Star Compass for Q2 2026 prioritizes LFW partner channel growth and
  internal delivery scaling.

## Must-Include Slides

- The 30-second explanation.
- What partners ask the client for.
- The handoff sequence and partner/LFW ownership boundary.
- What the client receives in the report.
- When to use VentureForge and when to skip straight to ForgeWorks.
- How the report turns into the next decision.

## Risks / Constraints

- Keep claims source-backed or label them as positioning.
- Do not make "AI-powered" the argument.
- Avoid making the free analysis sound like a bait-and-switch.
- The deck should make the next client conversation easier, not explain every
  implementation detail.
